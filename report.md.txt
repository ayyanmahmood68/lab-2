### Exercise 3: Critique of Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) on Small Samples
- **Analysis:** Fitting a continuous Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) curve over a very small sample size ($N=4$) is statistically misleading. Small samples are highly sensitive to bandwidth choices and exhibit massive variance, creating artificial peaks.
- **Alternative:** Discrete strip plots, rug plots, or empirical summary tables should be used instead of continuous distributions for micro-corpora[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
